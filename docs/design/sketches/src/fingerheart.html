Finger heart — closer look

The 🫰 gesture as the “like”: thumb crossed over the index finger with a little heart popping out. It reads as affection rather than approval, it’s popular across Korea/East Asia and spreading globally through K-pop, and it isn’t owned by any big platform. Variants mix in the bee/hive idea.
