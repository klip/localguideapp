BeLikeLocal — logo v3

No hearts. The crossed-fingers gesture stays (it says “nice / like” in a friendly way) and a small bee flies off it, so “Be” becomes the bee. A hand-drawn honeycomb sits behind it as the brand’s texture. Redrawn from the real gesture: the thumb is the big finger pointing up, the tip of the index peeks out behind it, and the other fingers curl into the palm. Wordmark in CamelCase.
