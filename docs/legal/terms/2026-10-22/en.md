## 2. What the platform does

BeLikeLocal is a meeting place. It helps travellers and guides find each other, show mutual interest, talk through the platform's chat and book a meeting. Any tour, activity or other service is agreed directly between the traveller and the guide. We are not a party to that agreement. We do not employ guides or act as their agent, and we do not organise, supervise or check the services they provide.

### 2.1 Accounts and roles

One account can be a traveller, a guide or both. You choose the role you start with and can add the other later by accepting its agreement. You act in one role at a time; your profile text, interests, matches, bookings, reviews and level are kept separately for each role.

### 2.2 Finding each other

Travellers browse guides and guides browse travellers, one profile at a time, and choose "Interested" or "Reject". Profiles are ordered by an automatic ranking that takes into account how close the person is to the place you search, their reviews, their level, whether their identity has been verified, whether their profile is complete and how new their account is. Badges show the facts behind it (verified, top rated, trips completed, level). Nobody can pay for a better place.

You can search near a place: a guide sets the area where they guide and how far they travel; a traveller can set where they are looking, which guides see as the town only. Travellers can also plan a trip with several stops; the planner suggests guides for each stop with the help of an AI service (section 9.9), and guides see the town and dates of a traveller's upcoming stops, never more.

### 2.3 Picks, matches and chat

A traveller shows interest using a pick from a selection pack (section 5.2). When both sides are interested, it is a match. Matched people can write to each other and share a location in the chat. Contact details are never shared: the platform automatically hides phone numbers, email addresses, links and social media handles in what users write (section 4). When a match ends, the conversation stays readable but no new messages can be sent.

### 2.4 Calendars and reminders

A guide can keep a calendar with their working hours, days off and bookings. A traveller booking that guide can only choose a time when the guide is free. The platform sends notifications in the app and by email about requests, bookings, changes, reminders before a trip and the outcome of a trip.

### 2.5 Account security

Signing in may require a code sent by email, always when we do not recognise the browser. You can see the browsers signed in to your account, sign any of them out, and sign out everywhere. We may email you about a new sign-in or a suspicious one, with a link to secure your account.

## 5. Fees, bookings and cancellations

Prices for selection packs and guide fees are shown before you pay. The rules below, and the amounts shown in the app before each step, form part of these terms.

### 5.1 Protected by BeLikeLocal

Bookings made through BeLikeLocal (and paid here, where the guide charges a fee) are protected:

- Refunds by fixed rules (section 5.5): in full if you withdraw before the guide accepts, if the guide declines or doesn't answer in time, if the guide cancels (plus 5% when they cancel late) or if the trip is never started; the fee minus 5% if you cancel before the start and the guide agrees.
- A fair process when something goes wrong after the start: the guide confirms your cancellation, or our team reviews the trip and decides the refund (section 5.7).
- Help with complaints: we can look at the booking, its meeting place, how the start was confirmed and your messages on the platform (section 6).
- Honest reviews: only people who actually finished a trip together can review each other.

Arrangements made outside the platform are not protected: if you pay a guide directly or move the conversation elsewhere, we cannot refund you or help with a dispute, and that trip does not count towards reviews, levels or your place in search results.

### 5.2 Selection packs and picks

A selection pack costs £5 and holds 5 picks, plus one for each traveller level, up to 10. Choosing "Interested" on a guide uses a pick; rejecting is free. A pick is returned if you withdraw your interest before the guide answers, or if a booking with that guide is cancelled. Picks do not expire. Packs are not refundable once a pick has been used. Some traveller levels unlock a few days of unlimited free picks (section 5.9).

### 5.3 Booking a guide

A guide who charges is booked by paying their fee in the app, for a meeting time (a free time in their calendar, if they keep one), an optional trip length and an optional meeting place. The request then waits for the guide:

- the guide accepts it, and it becomes a confirmed trip, or declines it;
- you can withdraw it while it waits;
- if the guide hasn't answered a day before the meeting, we remind them; 15 minutes before the meeting both of you are warned; at the meeting time an unanswered request is cancelled.

A guide who guides for free has no fee: the trip is confirmed as soon as you match, and the two of you agree the time in the chat. Either side can set or change the meeting place, and the other is told. We remind both of you a day and two hours before a confirmed trip.

### 5.4 Starting a trip

The guide starts the trip in the app when you meet. The "Start the trip" button opens 15 minutes before the meeting time. While the trip is about to start and the app is open, both apps share the device's location with us every 30 seconds, so the platform can confirm you are together: the button unlocks only when both locations put you within 50 metres of each other. If either location is missing, out of date or not precise enough, both of you are asked to confirm the meetup instead, and the button unlocks once both have. The traveller is told when the trip has started. Locations used for this are never shown to the other person (section 9.2).

### 5.5 Cancellations and refunds

- You withdraw a request before the guide accepts: full refund.
- The guide declines, or doesn't answer by the meeting time: full refund.
- The guide cancels a confirmed trip: full refund, plus 5% of the fee, paid by the guide, when they cancel less than 24 hours before the meeting (or less than 2 hours before it if you matched less than a day earlier).
- You ask to cancel a confirmed trip before it starts: you give a reason; if the guide agrees you are refunded the fee minus a 5% cancellation fee, shown in the app before you ask; if the guide refuses, the booking stands.
- After the start: see section 5.7.
- Cancelling a trip ends the match on both sides and returns the traveller's pick. A free trip can be cancelled at any time by unmatching.

### 5.6 Trips that never start

A confirmed trip that nobody started by the end of its time — the meeting time plus its length, or three hours if no length was given — is cancelled automatically and the traveller is refunded in full straight away. It moves to your past trips, and you stay matched, so you can book another date.

### 5.7 Cancelling after the start

If the guide has started the trip and you didn't actually meet, or something else went wrong, you can cancel it in the app with a reason. There is no automatic refund: if the guide agrees, you are refunded in full; if the guide refuses, or doesn't answer within 24 hours, the trip goes to our team as a dispute. We review the booking, how the start was confirmed and your messages, and decide the refund, from nothing to the full fee. Both of you are told the decision. A disputed trip cannot finish or be reviewed until it is decided.

### 5.8 Finishing a trip and reviews

A trip with a known length finishes automatically when that length has passed since its start; otherwise either side marks it finished and the other confirms. After a finished trip you can review each other once per trip with a grade from 1 to 5 and an optional comment of up to 256 characters, which you can edit or delete. Reviews are kept per role, and a profile's rating comes only from them.

### 5.9 Levels, platform fees and bonuses

Each role has a level, from 0 (New) to 7 (Icon), earned only from paid, finished trips: one point per trip as a traveller, between one and one and a half per trip as a guide depending on the fee. Levels 1 to 7 need 1, 3, 6, 12, 24, 48 and 96 points. A guide who has only guided for free is shown as a Volunteer.

- Travellers get one more pick per pack for each level (up to 10), and at levels 2, 4 and 6 a bonus of 3, 7 or 14 days of unlimited free picks.
- Guides pay a platform fee on each paid booking, taken from the fee: 15% at levels 0 and 1, then 14%, 12%, 10%, 8%, 6% and 5% at level 7. It is fixed when the booking is paid and shown to the guide before they accept. Levels 4 and 6, and 10, 25 and 50 different reviewers averaging at least 4.8 stars, each give a bonus of fee-free days.

You start a bonus yourself from your profile. Our team may adjust a level or award a bonus or badge, and sanctions can lower a level and pause bonuses (section 6).

We will tell you about a decision that affects your account and why, unless the law or the safety of others prevents it, and you can ask us to review it by replying to that message.

We decide disputes about a started trip as described in section 5.7. Otherwise we are not obliged to settle disputes between users, including about the quality of a service, but we will try to help where we reasonably can.
