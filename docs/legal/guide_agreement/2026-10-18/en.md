# Guide Agreement

Version {{effective_date}}

This agreement applies when you guide through BeLikeLocal, operated by {{operator_name}}. It adds to the Terms and Conditions, which still apply in full. If the two conflict, the Terms and Conditions prevail.

## 1. You are independent

You guide on your own account. You are not an employee, agent or partner of BeLikeLocal. You decide what you offer, when and at what price, and you are responsible for any licence, permit, insurance and tax your guiding requires where you work.

## 2. Your profile and prices

Describe yourself, your tours, what is included and your price truthfully, and keep it up to date. Your headline and description as a guide are shown to visitors only. You may guide for free; a free guide is shown as a Volunteer until their first paid trip.

## 3. Bookings

A paid booking only becomes a trip when you accept it. Accept only what you can honour. Answer requests before the meeting time; a request nobody answers is declined automatically and the visitor is refunded.

## 4. Fees and payouts

Visitors pay your fee through BeLikeLocal. We keep a platform fee, shown before you accept, which falls as your guide level rises. Your level grows only from paid trips, and slightly faster for higher fees.

## 5. Cancellations

If you cancel a confirmed booking, the visitor gets their fee back. If you cancel less than 24 hours before the meeting, or less than 2 hours before it when the match is under a day old, they also receive an extra 5% of the fee, paid out of your earnings.

## 6. Conduct and safety

Treat visitors with respect and keep them safe. Never harass, discriminate against or mislead anyone. Keep contact and payment on BeLikeLocal: do not share phone numbers, emails, links or handles, and never take payment outside the platform.

## 7. Reviews

After a trip, visitors can review you and you can review them. Reviews must be honest. Your rating as a guide is separate from your rating as a traveller.

## 8. Ending this agreement

You can stop guiding at any time. We may suspend your guide profile, lower your level or end this agreement if you break it or the Terms and Conditions.
