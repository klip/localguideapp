# Guide Agreement

Version {{effective_date}}

This agreement applies when you guide through BeLikeLocal, operated by {{operator_name}}. It adds to the Terms and Conditions, which still apply in full. If the two conflict, the Terms and Conditions prevail.

## 1. You are independent

You guide on your own account. You are not an employee, agent or partner of BeLikeLocal. You decide what you offer, when and at what price, and you are responsible for any licence, permit, insurance and tax your guiding requires where you work.

## 2. Your profile, area and calendar

Describe yourself, your tours, what is included and your price truthfully, and keep it up to date. Your headline and description as a guide are shown to travellers only, with the town where you guide. You may guide for free; a free guide is shown as a Volunteer until their first paid trip. If you keep a calendar, keep it accurate: travellers can only book the free times in it.

## 3. Bookings

A paid booking only becomes a trip when you accept it. Accept only what you can honour. Answer requests before the meeting time: we remind you a day before and warn you 15 minutes before, and at the meeting time an unanswered request is cancelled and the traveller refunded in full. A free trip is confirmed as soon as you match.

## 4. Starting the trip

You start the trip in the app when you meet the traveller. The button opens 15 minutes before the meeting time and unlocks only when the app can confirm you are together: both locations within 50 metres, or — if a location is off or not precise enough — once you both confirm the meetup. Keep the app open around the meeting time. Never start a trip, or confirm a meetup, that didn't happen.

## 5. Fees and payouts

Travellers pay your fee through BeLikeLocal. We keep a platform fee from it, fixed when the booking is paid and shown before you accept: 15% at guide levels 0 and 1, falling to 5% at level 7. Some levels and outstanding reviews give you days without a platform fee. Your level grows only from paid, finished trips, slightly faster for higher fees.

## 6. Cancellations and refunds

- If you cancel a confirmed booking, the traveller gets their fee back. If you cancel less than 24 hours before the meeting, or less than 2 hours before it when the match is under a day old, they also receive an extra 5% of the fee, paid out of your earnings.
- If a traveller asks to cancel before the start and you agree, they are refunded the fee minus 5%; if you refuse, the booking stands.
- A trip you never start is cancelled when its time is over and the traveller is refunded in full.
- If the traveller cancels after you started the trip, you approve it (full refund) or refuse it. If you refuse, or don't answer within 24 hours, our team reviews the trip and decides the refund.

## 7. Conduct and safety

Treat travellers with respect and keep them safe. Never harass, discriminate against or mislead anyone. Keep contact and payment on BeLikeLocal: do not share phone numbers, emails, links or handles, and never take payment outside the platform.

## 8. Reviews

After a finished trip, travellers can review you and you can review them. Reviews must be honest. Your rating as a guide is separate from your rating as a traveller.

## 9. Ending this agreement

You can stop guiding at any time. We may suspend your guide profile, lower your level, pause your bonuses or end this agreement if you break it or the Terms and Conditions.
