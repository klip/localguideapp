# Traveller Agreement

Version {{effective_date}}

This agreement applies when you look for guides through BeLikeLocal, operated by {{operator_name}}. It adds to the Terms and Conditions, which still apply in full. If the two conflict, the Terms and Conditions prevail.

## 1. What you are agreeing to

Guides are independent. BeLikeLocal helps you find them, but the tour itself is agreed between you and the guide. Check what is included before you book.

## 2. Picks and packs

Showing interest in a guide uses a pick from a selection pack you buy. Packs are not refundable once used. A higher traveller level gives more picks per pack.

## 3. Booking and paying

Pay a guide's fee only through BeLikeLocal. A booking becomes a trip when the guide accepts it; until then you can withdraw it for a full refund. If the guide declines or does not answer in time, you are refunded in full.

## 4. Cancellations

Once a guide has accepted, you can ask to cancel before the trip starts. If the guide agrees, you get the fee back less 5%. If the guide cancels, you get the full fee back, plus 5% when they cancel late.

## 5. Your profile

Your headline and description as a traveller are shown to guides only. Keep them truthful, and never include contact details.

## 6. Conduct and safety

Treat guides with respect, turn up on time and follow their safety instructions. Meet in public places first, tell someone where you are going, and keep contact and payment on BeLikeLocal.

## 7. Reviews and levels

After a trip, you and the guide can review each other honestly. Your rating as a traveller is separate from your rating as a guide. Your traveller level grows only from paid trips.

## 8. Ending this agreement

You can stop at any time. We may suspend your account if you break this agreement or the Terms and Conditions.
