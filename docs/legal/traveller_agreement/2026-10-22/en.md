# Traveller Agreement

Version {{effective_date}}

This agreement applies when you look for guides through BeLikeLocal, operated by {{operator_name}}. It adds to the Terms and Conditions, which still apply in full. If the two conflict, the Terms and Conditions prevail.

## 1. What you are agreeing to

Guides are independent. BeLikeLocal helps you find them, but the tour itself is agreed between you and the guide. Check what is included, the price and the meeting place before you book.

## 2. Picks and packs

Showing interest in a guide uses a pick from a selection pack you buy (£5; 5 picks, plus one per traveller level, up to 10). Rejecting is free. A pick comes back if you withdraw your interest before the guide answers, or if a booking with that guide is cancelled. Packs are not refundable once a pick has been used.

## 3. Booking and paying

Pay a guide's fee only through BeLikeLocal. Choose a meeting time — a free time in the guide's calendar, if they keep one — and, if you like, a trip length and meeting place. The booking becomes a trip when the guide accepts it. Until then you can withdraw it for a full refund. If the guide declines, or hasn't answered by the meeting time, it is cancelled and you are refunded in full. We remind you a day and two hours before a trip.

## 4. Meeting and starting the trip

Your guide starts the trip in the app when you meet. Keep BeLikeLocal open around the meeting time: while the trip is about to start, the app shares your location with us so it can confirm you are together (within 50 metres). If your location is off or not precise enough, you and the guide confirm the meetup instead. Only confirm a meetup that really happened.

## 5. Cancellations

- Before the trip starts, you can ask the guide to cancel, with a reason. If they agree, you are refunded the fee minus a 5% cancellation fee, shown before you ask. If they refuse, the booking stands.
- If the guide cancels, you get the full fee back, plus 5% when they cancel late.
- If the trip is never started, it is cancelled when its time is over and you are refunded in full automatically.
- If the guide started the trip and you didn't meet, or something went wrong, cancel it in the app with a reason. If the guide agrees you are refunded in full; otherwise our team reviews it and decides the refund.

## 6. Your profile

Your headline and description as a traveller are shown to guides only. Guides also see the town and dates of where you are looking and of your planned trip stops. Keep your profile truthful, and never include contact details.

## 7. Conduct and safety

Treat guides with respect, turn up on time and follow their safety instructions. Meet in public places first, tell someone where you are going, and keep contact and payment on BeLikeLocal.

## 8. Reviews and levels

After a finished trip, you and the guide can review each other honestly. Your rating as a traveller is separate from your rating as a guide. Your traveller level grows only from paid, finished trips and gives you more picks per pack and, at some levels, days of free picks.

## 9. Ending this agreement

You can stop at any time. We may lower your level, pause your bonuses or suspend your account if you break this agreement or the Terms and Conditions.
